# TABLE VII.

# AGE, SEX AND CIVIL CONDITION.

This table consists of two parts; Part A gives the provincial summary of the whole population by religion, age, sex and civil condition and Part B contains the distribution by districts.

This table corresponds with Table VII of 1921 but the method of compiling the quinary age groups differs from that employed at the 1921 census. The method used at the present census is, briefly, as follows:—The ages returned were first grouped in alternate groups of 3 and 7, e.g., 4-6 and 7-13 and then regrouped into quinary groups by halving each group and adding half the subsequent or preceding group.
