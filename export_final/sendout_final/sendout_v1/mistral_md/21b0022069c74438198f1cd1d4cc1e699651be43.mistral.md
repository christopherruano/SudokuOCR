# IMPERIAL TABLE VIII.

## Civil Condition by Age for Selected Races.

NOTES 1. In this table figures are given not for the whole population but for certain selected races or racial groups in the districts and states where they are specially numerous. All the races are indigenous with the exception of Telugu.

2. In Imperial Table VII figures have been given for the usual five-yearly groups and in Note 4 to that table on page 53 the manner in which these figures were obtained has been described. In this table the figures have not been adjusted to form five-yearly groups since by leaving them in groups of 3 and 7 it is possible to give figures for the ages 14-16, which were considered of importance. It should be noted that the age-group 14-16 includes those who had completed 13½ but had not completed 16½ years; the other age-groups have a similar interpretation.
