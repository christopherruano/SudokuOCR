## TABLE VII.

### Age, Sex and Civil Condition.

NOTE 1.—This Table is divided into three parts. Part I shows the distribution for the State as a whole of the total population and of each religion by age, sex and civil condition. Part II shows the distribution in districts of the total population and of the main religions by age, sex and civil condition. Part III gives similar details for Cities.

NOTE 2.—The age recorded at this census was the age nearest birthday and not the number of years completed as at the last census. The numbers recorded were grouped into tenary and septenary groups and then re-distributed in the ordinary quinary groups. This re-distribution was first effected for the districts and the smoothed figures were then consolidated for the State.
