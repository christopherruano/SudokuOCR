Hyderabad Census, 1931

### Table VII.

# AGE, SEX AND CIVIL CONDITION

Note.—This table is divided into three parts and shows the distribution of the population by age, sex and civil condition. Part I shows the distribution for the State as a whole of the total population, male and female, by each religion; part II shows similar statistics of the total district population and for main religion only and part III gives similar information for the four cities.

2. The ages shown in this table are smoothed according to the method recommended by the Actuary to the Government of India,—the method being conversion of ternary and septenary groups into quinary groups. The individual ages, reckoned in the present Census are from the nearest birthday and not (like 1921) from the number of completed years.
