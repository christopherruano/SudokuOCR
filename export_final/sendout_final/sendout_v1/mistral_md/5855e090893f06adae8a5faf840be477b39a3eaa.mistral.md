# TABLE VII.

## Age, Sex and Civil Condition by Religions.

The ages of the people returned represent the actual number of years completed, not the current year of age.

According to the instructions each person, whether infant, child or grown-up, was returned as either married, unmarried or widowed. Divorced persons were returned as widowed. A woman who has never been married was entered as unmarried, though she was a prostitute or was living with a man. Persons who were recognised by custom as married were entered as such even though they had not gone through the full ceremony.

This Table is divided into two parts. Part I contains the statistics for India for the total population and for each main religion. The Jewish religion has been included in Minor and Unspecified. Part II gives the statistics for the total population and three largest religions for each of the 17 principal Provinces, States and Agencies.

The population dealt with in this Table falls short of that returned in Tables I and VI by 983,756 persons enumerated in tracts where age and civil condition were not recorded.
