# IMPERIAL TABLE VII.

## Age, Sex and Civil Condition.

The age shown in this table represents the age as it was or would have been on the birthday nearest to the date of enumeration; that is, to the nearest approximate number of years. Divorced persons have been entered as "Widowed". Persons who are recognized by custom as married have been shown as such even though they have not gone through any recognized formal ceremony.

2. The table is divided into three parts. Part A shows the distribution for the Agency as a whole of the total population and of the population of each religion returned. Part B shows the distribution of the population in principal States for main religions, but in this part the age statistics have been given in less detail than in the Agency summary. Part C gives similar details for Cities.
