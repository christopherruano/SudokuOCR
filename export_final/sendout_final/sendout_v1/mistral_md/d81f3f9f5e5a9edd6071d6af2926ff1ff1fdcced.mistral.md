Hyderabad Census, 1931

### Table VIII.

# AGE, SEX AND CIVIL CONDITION FOR SELECTED CASTES, RACES AND TRIBES

NOTE :— 1. This table does not deal with the whole population; but with certain castes, races and tribes for which details cannot be obtained from the Imperial Table VII. The selection of a caste is either based on account of its numerical strength or its local importance.

2. Figures are given here for the State as a whole and for the two natural divisions, viz., Telangana (including Hyderabad City) and Marathwara.
3. The age-periods as shown in this table are as obtained from the actual returns in the Enumeration Books and hence cannot collaborate with the age-periods in the Imperial Table VII, which have been smoothed into quinary groups.
4. The attention of the reader is drawn to the Imperial Table XVII (Fly-leaf) for those castes which have been grouped into one familiar caste name.
