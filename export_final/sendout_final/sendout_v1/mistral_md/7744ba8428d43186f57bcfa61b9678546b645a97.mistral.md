## TABLE VII.

AGE, SEX AND CIVIL CONDITION.

1. The age shown in this table represents the number of years completed and not the current year of age.
2. This table is divided into three parts. Part A shows the distribution for the Punjab as a whole, for British Territory, for Punjab States (both groups), and for Delhi of the total population and of each main religion by age, sex and civil condition. Part B shows the distribution for Districts and States, of the total population and of the five principal religions (Hindu, Musalman, Christian, Jain and Sikh) by age, sex and civil condition. Part C gives similar details with the addition of figures for "Others" (other religions which include Buddhist, Parsi and Jew) for cities and selected towns.

Note.—Age statistics for selected tahsils and places treated as towns for Census purposes are given in Part III (Provincial Tables).
