# Imperial Table VII

(2) Sample slips of married females as a whole.

The following seven Fertility Tables have been constructed:—

*1. Table I .. Size of family.
2. Table II .. Sex of the first born.
3. Table III .. Size of family according to community.
4. Table IV. .. Size of family by age of mother at birth of first child.

*5. Table V .. Age of mother at birth of first child and number of children born.

*6. Table VI .. Age of mother at birth of first child and number of children surviving.

7. Table VII .. Number of children born and surviving.

*The basis of the figures in these tables are the slips for completed fertility.
