106. Comparison by Age and Sex.—In the accompanying abstract is shown, (1) the distribution of 1,000 persons of unsound mind by age, and (2) the proportion of afflicted to the total number of persons at each age-period. It will be seen that it is in the prime of life that insanity is most prevalent, and that it attacks females at a much earlier age than males. More than a fourth of the insane of the female sex are girls or young women between the ages of 10 and 20 years. From the proportions given in columns 4 and 5 we gather that the value of the life of the insane decreases with advancing years at a faster rate than that of the general population. At all ages there are more persons of unsound mind among males than among females; the average of the latter tends, however, to rise in old age.
