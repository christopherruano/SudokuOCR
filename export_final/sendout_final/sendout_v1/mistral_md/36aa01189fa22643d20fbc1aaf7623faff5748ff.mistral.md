# TABLE XIV

# CIVIL CONDITION BY AGE FOR SELECTED CASTES.

THIS table contains statistics of civil condition by age for selected castes or tribes, whereas Table VII (Age, Sex and Civil Condition) deals with the total population. There is this further difference between the two tables, that the age periods are not the same, the periods 5-12 and 12-15 being substituted in this table for the periods 5-10 and 10-15 given in Table VII. As in Table IX, the selected castes and tribes are grouped together for Calcutta and the suburban municipalities.
