### TABLE VII.

### Age, Sex and Civil Condition.

*Explanatory Note.*

(1) This Table is divided into three parts, viz:—

Part I.—The Summary for the Whole State. It shows distribution of the total population and each religion in annual groups from 0—5, then the total of these and after this total quinquennial groups from 5 to 70 and lastly 70 and over. It may be noted that Aryas are included in Hindus according to the instructions received from the Census Superintendent, Rajputana.

Part II.—Details by Nizamats and Thikanas. Age groups are arranged by religions from 0—1 and 1—5; then quinquennial from 5—20, and after this decennial from 20—60, the last group being 60 and over.

Part III.—Distribution of Population by Religion for the City of Jaipur only. Age groups are the same as in Part II (in “All Religions” the total of 0—1 and 1—5 is also given).

(2) The figures showing the age of the people represent the number of years to nearest birthday or the nearest age of the people enumerated on the Census day. Divorced persons (males and females) have been included in the heading “Widowed,” provided that they were not re-married. Prostitutes and Concubines are also entered as unmarried.

(3) The term “Tribal” is used instead of “Animist” and “Zoroastrian” instead of “Parsi”, “Muslim” instead of Musalman or Mohammedan.
