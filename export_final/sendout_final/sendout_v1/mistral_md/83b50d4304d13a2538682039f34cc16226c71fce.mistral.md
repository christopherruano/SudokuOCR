# BARODA CENSUS, 1931

# TABLE VII—Age, Sex and Civil Condition
(In Three Parts)

NOTE 1: Part A shows the distribution for the State as a whole of the total population and each religion by Age, Sex and Civil Condition. Part B shows the distribution for divisions of the total population and of the main religions by Age, Sex and Civil Condition. Part C gives similar details for the City of Baroda.

NOTE 2: The ages shown in this Table are smoothed according to the method prescribed in para 7 Chapter IV of the Imperial Code of Census Procedure 1931-Part II. It is the method of re-distribution of ternary and septenary groups into quinary groups, as recommended by the Actuary to the Government of India.
