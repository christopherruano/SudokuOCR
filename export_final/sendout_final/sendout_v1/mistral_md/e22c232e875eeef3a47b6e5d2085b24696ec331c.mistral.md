# TABLE VIII

# Civil Condition by Age for Selected Castes.

This table does not give statistics for the Agency as a whole. A few castes of local importance have been chosen from some of the principal States. The total figures for Banias, Brahmans and Rajputs and for Muslims, where given, do not represent the full strength of the caste or religion but only the total of the main sub-castes or classes that were selected for the table. In most cases Hindus and Jains have been combined under Banias and Hindus and Tribals under Tribal groups, such as, Bhil, Bhilala, Gond, Kol and Korku.
