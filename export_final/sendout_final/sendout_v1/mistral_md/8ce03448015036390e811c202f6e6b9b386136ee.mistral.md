### INFIRMITIES BY CASTE, ETC.

116. The castes of the infirm are set forth in four long tables, and it is by no means an easy matter to deal with the large amount of detail which has there been collected. Provincial figures showing the proportion of the afflicted in the more important castes have been worked out and are printed in this chapter, but in the case of infirmities which are perhaps more local than racial, such as blindness and leprosy, it is evident that unless the proportions for different districts are taken into account, the results will have but little meaning. This must, however, be left for more specialized inquiry, as the space at my disposal will hardly permit of such detailed treatment of the subject.
