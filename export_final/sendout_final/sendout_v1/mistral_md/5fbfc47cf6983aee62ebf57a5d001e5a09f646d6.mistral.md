"Formula for Correction Factors to deduce more accurate population figures from the figures given in the imperial table VII after preliminary adjustment.

Let P₁₀₋₁₅ denote the population of the age group—"10-15," i.e., the number of persons who have completed the age of 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14 years as shown in imperial table VII after preliminary adjustments for minor misstatements of age by the method prescribed;

P'₁₀₋₁₅ denote the population of the same age-group—"10-15" after further adjustments to correct the error due to continuous decrement for mortality;

M₁₀ denote the rate of mortality at the age 10, which may be taken from the tables on pages 20-21 of Mr. Meikle's "Report of 1926 on the age distribution, etc.," deduced from the Indian Census of 1921, etc.," more recent mortality rates not being available now; and

M₁₀₋₁₅ denote the mean of the rates of mortality at the ages 10 and 15 being equal to ½(M₁₀+M₁₅).

Then P'₀₋₅, P'₅₋₁₀, etc., would be as follows:—

$$\mathrm{P}'_{0·5} = \mathrm{P}_{0·5} + \frac{1}{2} \times \mathrm{M}_5 \times \mathrm{P}_{5·10} \tag{1}$$

$$\mathrm{P}'_{5·10} = \mathrm{P}_{5·10} + \mathrm{M}_{10·15} \times \mathrm{P}_{10·15} - \frac{1}{2} \times \mathrm{M}_5 \times \mathrm{P}_{5·10} \tag{2}$$

$$\mathrm{P}'_{10·15} = \mathrm{P}_{10·15} - \mathrm{M}_{10·15} \times \mathrm{P}_{15·15} \tag{3}$$

$$\mathrm{P}'_{15·20} = \mathrm{P}_{15·20} + \mathrm{M}_{20·25} \times \mathrm{P}_{20·25} \tag{4}$$

$$\mathrm{P}'_{20·25} = \mathrm{P}_{20·25} - \mathrm{M}_{20·25} \times \mathrm{P}_{20·25} \tag{5}$$

$$\mathrm{P}'_{55·60} = \mathrm{P}_{55·60} + \mathrm{M}_{60·65} \times \mathrm{P}_{60·65} \tag{6}$$

$$\mathrm{P}'_{60·65} = \mathrm{P}_{60·65} - \mathrm{M}_{60·65} \times \mathrm{P}_{60·65} \tag{7}$$

$$\mathrm{P}'_{65·70} = \mathrm{P}_{65·70} + \mathrm{M}_{65} \times \mathrm{P}_{70} \text{ and over} \tag{8}$$

$$\mathrm{P}'_{70} \text{ and over} = \mathrm{P}_{70} \text{ and over} - \mathrm{M}_{65} \times \mathrm{P}_{70} \text{ and over} \tag{9}$$

NOTE 1.—If P (Population) be expressed in hundreds, M should be mortality rate per cent.

NOTE 2.—The 'Mortality Tables on pages 20-21 of Mr. Meikle's Report of 1926 on the Age Distribution do not give the rates of mortality at more than 65 years of age. Hence M₆₅ has been used in formula (8) and (9) above.
