In the sequence of themes arranged in the order of the Imperial tables, this review proposes to divide the total population returned at the last census into 3 main classes, viz., (1) the single or unmarried, (2) the married and (3) the widowed. The imperfections of the enumeration have added a fourth division to the above, viz., the unspecified.

Table VIII accordingly shows for each District and separately for each religion the number of persons of each sex who were at the time of the census single (unmarried), married and widowed, combined with their age-periods.
