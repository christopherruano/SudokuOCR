# APPENDIX TO IMPERIAL TABLE XIV.

## CIVIL CONDITION BY AGE AND CASTE OF CERTAIN SECTS IN SELECTED DISTRICTS.

1. This table has been prepared according to Census Commissioner's instructions contained in his Nos. 1227 and 1255, dated the 25th August 1911, and 29th August 1911, respectively, and shows for certain districts, the Civil Condition by Age, of the castes of Aryas, Brahmos and the followers of Dev Dharm (vide para. 4, Title page of Imperial Table XIV).
2. The Districts selected are those where the followers of these sects are found in considerable strength.
3. Only 40 Sikhs have returned themselves as Aryas and 22 as Brahmos in these Districts. They have not, therefore, been included in the figures shown in this table.
4. Castes returning less than 10 persons have been grouped under 'Others.'
