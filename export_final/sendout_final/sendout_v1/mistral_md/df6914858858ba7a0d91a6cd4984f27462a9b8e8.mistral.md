### TABLE VII

Age, Sex and Civil Condition

1. This Table is divided into three parts. Part A shows the distribution for the Province as a whole of the total population and of each religion by age, sex and civil condition. Part B shows the distribution in Districts and States of the total population and of the main religions by age, sex and civil condition, but in this part the age statistics have been given in less detail than in the Provincial Summary. Part C gives similar details for cities.
2. Detailed information for the age periods not shown in Parts B and C, can be obtained from the manuscript Compilation Registers which have been preserved.
3. The method of grouping ages is that recommended by the Actuary to the Government of India, and age groups have been smoothed in order to secure greater accuracy, according to the system laid down in the Imperial Census Code.
