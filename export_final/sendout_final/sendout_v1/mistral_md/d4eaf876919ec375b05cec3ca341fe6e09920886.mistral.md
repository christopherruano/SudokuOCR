CIVIL CONDITION.

[Chap. V.

The low proportion of the married among Kodagus, between 10 and 15, shows that marriage before puberty is rare with them. It is also fairly uncommon among the Kurubas, Native Christians, Vakkaligas and Lingáyats. It is, of course, most extensively practised by Bráhmans. The proportion of widows at 15 to 39 affords some indication of the extent to which the prohibition of the marriage of widows prevails. It is absolutely forbidden among Bráhmans and it is not usual, even if not prohibited, among Dévángas, Lingáyats and Vakkaligas. Among Yeravas, Kodagus, Kurubas and Páles it is both allowed and practised to a not inconsiderable extent.
