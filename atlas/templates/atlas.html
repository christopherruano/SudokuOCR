How to read this map. Each modern district (GADM 4.1) shows what the digitized census holds for the historical district or state that covered it — whether that district had its own table or only appears as a row inside a provincial one. Self-checked means its age bands add up to the printed total (or to 1,000/10,000 for proportional tables), so the figure is internally verified. On the indicator layers, districts without a local figure show their province's value pale with a grey outline: a regional value, not a local one. Scales are fixed across years so colours are comparable. Historical→modern links: crosswalk CSV; figures: indicators CSV; districts whose age tables are only partly captured: re-extraction list.
